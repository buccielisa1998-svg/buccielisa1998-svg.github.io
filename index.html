ONGOING · Ph.D. RESEARCH

Random forests through a stochastic-control lens

Studying mathematical perspectives on CART random forests: viewing feature subsampling as random sets of available choices and tree splitting as a sequence of allocation decisions. This framework helps connect local tree-building behavior to properties of the entire ensemble.

SUPERVISORProf. Jinchi Lv
